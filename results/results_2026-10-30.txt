   together, big updates flip the greedy choice).

   of an unstable agent, not a stable level.

   switches to protection at HF 1.3 (Check 3).

   but checkpoints still swing (up to 0.20), pro_gap only clear for seed 42,
   ctrl_gain 2.58-6.32.
5. Soft target updates (tau 0.005) on seed 1: better early (ep200-500 all 0),
   but still jumps at ep600-700 (0.28, 0.16); mean ep400+ 0.069 vs 0.077.
   -> no clear gain on one seed. Decision rule (set before the run): freeze.
6. FINAL SETUP (frozen for the ablation): LR 1e-4, Double DQN, no Huber,
   grad clip 10, hard target copy every 500 steps (USE_SOFT_TARGET = False).
   "full" group = full_seed42_lr1e4, full_seed1_lr1e4, full_seed2_lr1e4.
7. Lead for discussion (not proven): with LR 1e-4 the bad checkpoints cluster
   at ep600-800, when the 50k-step replay buffer (~555 episodes) starts deleting
